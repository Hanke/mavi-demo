Part-Time Bookkeeper for a Restaurant Group (Remote, Pacific hours)

Sunfish Hospitality runs four restaurants and a catering business in Portland, Oregon. We need a part-time bookkeeper, roughly 15 to 20 hours a week, to keep the books for all five entities in QuickBooks Online.

You will
- Reconcile bank, credit card and POS deposits weekly for five entities.
- Enter and pay vendor bills through Dext and QuickBooks Online; keep the AP aging clean.
- Run biweekly payroll for about 140 employees, including tips, in Gusto.
- File Oregon and Multnomah County tax reports and prepare quarterly sales summaries.
- Close each entity monthly and hand off to our CPA with a reconciliation package.

Required
- QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor certification.
- At least 3 years of bookkeeping for restaurants or other hospitality businesses.
- Hands-on experience with Gusto payroll, including tip reporting.
- Dext (formerly Receipt Bank) for receipt capture and bill entry.
- Able to work at least two mornings a week during Pacific time.

Bonus
- Experience with Toast or Square POS reconciliations.
- Xero experience (one of our entities may move to it).
- Comfortable talking to chefs and managers about invoices.

Start as soon as possible. $32 to $40 per hour depending on experience. Independent contractor or part-time employee, your choice.
