CONTROLLER
Hadley Precision Castings, Inc. - Toledo, Ohio - On-site

About us
Hadley Precision Castings is a family-owned investment casting foundry with 310 employees and about $95 million in annual sales to aerospace and industrial customers. We run SAP S/4HANA (implemented 2024) and report under US GAAP with a December year-end.

The role
The Controller reports to the CFO and leads a team of six covering general ledger, cost accounting, accounts payable, accounts receivable and payroll. You will own the monthly close, standard costing, the annual audit and the budget process.

What you will be accountable for
- A five-business-day close and monthly management accounts with variance commentary.
- Standard cost maintenance for about 900 part numbers, monthly PPV, absorption and scrap variance analysis, and the annual cost roll.
- Inventory controls: cycle counts, reserves for excess and obsolete stock, and the physical inventory.
- The annual financial statement audit and the bank covenant reporting package.
- Budgeting and quarterly forecasting with plant management.
- Sales and use tax, property tax and support for the outside tax preparer.

Must have
- CPA or CMA.
- 10 or more years of accounting experience with at least 5 in a manufacturing environment.
- Standard costing experience in a make-to-order or foundry setting.
- SAP experience (S/4HANA or ECC) as the system of record.
- Willingness to work on-site in Toledo five days a week.

Preferred
- Power BI or similar reporting tools.
- Experience with an ERP implementation or upgrade.
- Aerospace quality environment (AS9100) exposure.

Start: as soon as possible. Salary range $135,000 to $160,000 with annual bonus.
