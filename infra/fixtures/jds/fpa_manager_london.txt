FP&A Manager
Corrin Pay Ltd - London (Shoreditch), hybrid three days a week

- 6+ years in FP&A or commercial finance, including 2+ years managing people.
- Anaplan model-building experience (Level 2 or equivalent hands-on experience).
- Fintech or payments industry experience.
- Advanced Excel modelling and the ability to present to senior stakeholders.
- Right to work in the UK; based in or commutable to London.

Desirable
- SQL and experience querying a data warehouse directly.
- Power BI or Looker for management reporting.
- Experience in a business that has raised venture capital.

Salary £85,000 to £100,000 plus bonus and equity. Start date to be agreed, ideally within three months.
