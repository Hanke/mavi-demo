Financial Controller, North America
Orrin Analytics Inc. - Boston, MA (hybrid, two days a week in the office)

Orrin Analytics is the US arm of a UK-headquartered data software group. The North American business has $40M of revenue across two entities and is growing quickly, and the group is preparing for a US listing. We are hiring a Financial Controller to run accounting for the region and to work with the group finance team in London.

What you will do
- Own the month-end close for the two US entities in NetSuite and the reporting pack to the UK parent.
- Lead the conversion of group reporting from FRS 102 to US GAAP ahead of the planned listing.
- Manage and develop a team of three accountants.
- Run the annual audit and coordinate with the group's auditors in London.

Requirements
- CPA or equivalent international qualification (ACA, ACCA, CA).
- 8+ years of accounting experience, including group reporting for a software business.
- NetSuite as a daily user.
- Advanced Excel.

Preferred
- US GAAP reporting experience, including ASC 606 for subscription revenue.
- BlackLine.
- Experience working with a UK parent company.

Eastern time hours. Salary $150,000 to $175,000 plus bonus.
