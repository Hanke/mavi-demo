Senior Accountant (Hybrid, San Francisco)
Vantara Health Software | Finance & Accounting | Full-time

- Hands-on NetSuite experience as a daily user: journal imports, saved searches, multi-subsidiary consolidation.

- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP or INDEX-MATCH, Power Query).
- Located in the San Francisco Bay Area and able to work from our SoMa office Tuesday through Thursday; Pacific time hours.
- Able to start on or before Monday, November 2, 2026.

Preferred

- Experience with ASC 606 for multi-element SaaS contracts.
- Healthcare technology background.
