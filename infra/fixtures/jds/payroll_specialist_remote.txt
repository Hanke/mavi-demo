Senior Payroll Specialist (Remote - US)
Bluepeak Senior Living | People Operations

Bluepeak operates 41 assisted-living communities in 9 states with about 3,800 hourly and salaried employees. Our two-person payroll team runs biweekly payroll on ADP Workforce Now, and we are adding a third, senior member to take over tax and compliance.

Day to day
- Process biweekly payroll for roughly 3,800 employees, including shift differentials, overtime under state-specific rules and PTO accruals.
- Own multi-state payroll tax: registrations, quarterly reconciliations, notices and year-end W-2s.
- Reconcile payroll to the general ledger each cycle and prepare the payroll accrual journal entries.
- Audit time and attendance imports and resolve exceptions with community managers.
- Handle garnishments, verifications of employment and off-cycle payments.

You must have
- At least 3 years of end-to-end payroll processing on ADP Workforce Now.
- Multi-state payroll tax experience (we are in CA, WA, OR, AZ, CO, TX, FL, GA and NC).
- Experience with a workforce of 1,000 or more hourly employees.
- Strong Excel skills for reconciliations and audits.
- Availability during Eastern Time business hours (8am to 5pm ET), from anywhere in the US.

Nice to have
- CPP or FPC certification.
- Healthcare, senior living or hospitality payroll background.
- Experience with an ADP to Workday or UKG migration.

Start date: January 5, 2027. This is a fully remote, full-time position. Pay: $68,000 to $78,000.
